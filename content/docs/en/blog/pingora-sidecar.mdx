---
title: The lightweight Pole Sidecar data-plane path
description: How Pingora, HTTP/2, gRPC-h2c, routing, load balancing, and interceptors shape Pole Sidecar.
---

Pole Sidecar is intentionally smaller than a full service mesh. Its current Pingora-based implementation first establishes reliable protocol forwarding, route selection, round-robin balancing, and interceptor extension points.

That sequence creates a testable runtime boundary before dynamic governance is added. Each new policy should define its control-plane view, local state, request behavior, failure mode, and observability evidence.

Pole Sidecar must not become another control plane. Lattice Hub owns policy creation, versioning, progressive delivery, and rollback; Pole Sidecar executes the subset supported by its deployed version.

Future work should be described as a roadmap until the corresponding data-plane behavior is implemented and verified.
