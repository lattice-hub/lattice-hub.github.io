---
title: Synchronizing Kubernetes Service into Lattice Hub
description: Compare full, selective, and annotation-driven synchronization models.
---

Kubernetes Service synchronization answers three questions: what is authoritative, how names map to Lattice Hub environments and services, and how multiple clusters avoid ownership conflicts.

Full synchronization offers broad visibility but can import resources that do not need governance. Selective synchronization reduces noise but requires an explicit allowlist. Annotation-driven synchronization keeps intent near the workload while adding policy and validation requirements.

Whichever mode is selected, endpoint churn should update the same logical service rather than create unstable identities. Deletion must be reconciled explicitly, and controller restart should converge without duplicating records.

Begin with one namespace, test lifecycle events, and only then expand the synchronization boundary.
