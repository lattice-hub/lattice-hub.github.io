description: Reproducible performance, configuration, and verification evidence for Lattice Hub.

Reports contain verified facts, experiment conditions, and explicit gaps. They do not turn planned benchmarks or local observations into production claims.

Current entries:

- [Performance report](/en/docs/reports/performance): known runtime facts, measurement dimensions, and the benchmark plan.

Every result should identify the commit, topology, protocol, data set, load profile, duration, and collection method required to reproduce it.
